Speak-a-Speed

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026 (app version 0.4.0)

Speak-a-Speed does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no server, and no analytics of any kind. Your location and settings never leave your device.

Permissions Used

  • Location (Fine / Coarse / Background): used only to calculate your current speed from GPS while the app is running. Your location is processed entirely on your device to produce a speed value and is never recorded, transmitted, or shared with anyone, including us.
  • Foreground Service (incl. Location & Media Playback types): lets speed tracking and voice announcements continue while your screen is off or another app is in front, as required by Android for apps that track location in the background. A persistent notification is shown while this is active, as Android requires.
  • Notifications: used to display the required foreground-service notification described above.
  • Phone State: used only to detect when a call starts or ends, so announcements can be paused, continued, or muted according to your "During calls" setting. No call data (numbers, contacts, call history) is read, stored, or transmitted.
  • Wake Lock: used only to keep the screen on when you enable "Keep screen on" in Settings, and to keep GPS tracking active while announcements are running.
  • Receive Boot Completed: used only to resume speed tracking automatically if the foreground service was active when your device restarted. It does not run the app or collect data on every boot otherwise.

Data Storage

All settings (activity, units, voice, announcement thresholds, and similar preferences) are stored locally on your device only, using standard Android/iOS local storage. Nothing is synced to a cloud service, because Speak-a-Speed has no backend server at all.

Voice Announcements (Text-to-Speech)

Speed is spoken aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. Speak-a-Speed prefers voices that work fully offline. If you manually select a voice in your device's system Text-to-Speech settings that requires network access, speech synthesis for that voice may involve your operating system's TTS provider (e.g. Google Text-to-Speech) contacting the internet. That processing is performed by your device's system TTS engine, not by Speak-a-Speed, and is governed by that provider's own privacy policy — Speak-a-Speed itself never transmits your speed or location data anywhere.

Advertising & Monetisation

Speak-a-Speed does not currently display ads or require a subscription. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated first, and any such feature will be described here, including what (if anything) it collects.

Summary

Children's Privacy

Speak-a-Speed is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, regardless of age — because it does not collect data from any user at all.
